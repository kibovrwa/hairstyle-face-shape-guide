# Hairstyle by Face Shape: a Practical Checklist

A short, opinionated checklist for picking a haircut, hair color or beard before you sit in the chair. It is maintained by the team behind [EasyFace AI](https://easyfaceai.com/), an AI hairstyle, hair color and facial hair try-on. You upload one photo and preview a look on your own face. It does not do face swaps.

> TL;DR: work out your face shape, shortlist 3–4 cuts, then preview them on a well-lit photo of yourself before you commit. A cut you have seen on your own face is easier to describe to a barber or stylist.

## 1. Find your face shape

Pull your hair back, look straight at the camera, and compare forehead, cheekbone and jaw width with face length.

| Face shape | What you see | Cuts worth trying |
|---|---|---|
| Oval | Length a bit more than width, soft jaw | [Pixie](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/pixie), [lob](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/lob), [curtain bangs](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/curtain-bangs), [slicked back](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/slicked-back) |
| Round | Width ≈ length, full cheeks | [Layers](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/layers), [side part](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/side-part), [faux hawk](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/faux-hawk), [high ponytail](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/high-ponytail) |
| Square | Strong, angular jaw | [Soft waves](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/soft-waves), [shag](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/shag), [curtain bangs](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/curtain-bangs), [crew cut](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/crew-cut) |
| Heart | Wide forehead, narrow chin | [Lob](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/lob), [side part](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/side-part), [soft waves](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/soft-waves), [bangs](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/bangs) |
| Long | Length clearly more than width | [Bangs](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/bangs), [waves](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/waves), [lob](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/lob), [French crop](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/french-crop) |
| Diamond | Wide cheekbones, narrow forehead and jaw | [Pixie](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/pixie), [curtain bangs](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/curtain-bangs), [layers](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/layers), [tousled](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/tousled) |

These are starting points, not rules. Hairline, hair texture and the lighting in the photo matter as much as the shape. There is a longer write-up in the [face-shape hairstyle guide](https://easyfaceai.com/face-shape-hairstyles).

## 2. Shortlist before you preview

- **Going shorter?** Compare a [buzz cut](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/buzz-cut), [crew cut](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/crew-cut) and [French crop](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/french-crop) side by side. These are the hardest cuts to undo.
- **Want more movement?** Try a [wolf cut](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/wolf-cut), [shag](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/shag) or [curly hair](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/curly-hair).
- **Not sure about bangs?** Preview [curtain bangs](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/curtain-bangs) before blunt [bangs](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles/bangs).
- The full list of cuts is in the [hairstyle catalog](https://easyfaceai.com/hairstyles).

## 3. Color and facial hair

- Color changes the face as much as a cut does. Try [blonde](https://easyfaceai.com/hair-color/blonde), [black](https://easyfaceai.com/hair-color/black), [red](https://easyfaceai.com/hair-color/red) or [ombre](https://easyfaceai.com/hair-color/ombre) on the same photo. More options are on the [hair color page](https://easyfaceai.com/hair-color).
- Beard shape can balance a jaw. Compare [stubble](https://easyfaceai.com/facial-hair/stubble), a [full beard](https://easyfaceai.com/facial-hair/full-beard), a [goatee](https://easyfaceai.com/facial-hair/goatee) and a [Van Dyke](https://easyfaceai.com/facial-hair/van-dyke) in the [facial hair try-on](https://easyfaceai.com/facial-hair).

## 4. Take a photo that previews well

- Face the camera and use even, soft light. Avoid strong filters.
- JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 8 MB, with the short edge at least 256 px. HEIC photos are converted in the browser.
- Keep hair off the face if you want to see the hairline clearly.

## 5. Compare, then decide

Generate 3–4 options from the same photo and put them next to each other. The [compare page](https://easyfaceai.com/compare) explains how EasyFace stacks up against other try-on tools, and the [curated looks](https://easyfaceai.com/looks) are a quick place to start.

## Privacy notes

- The free preview is watermarked and at most 800 px on the long edge.
- Uploaded photos are deleted when the run finishes. Unpaid previews are deleted after 7 days and paid results after 30 days. Details are on [How it works](https://easyfaceai.com/how-it-works).
- If you need to hide a face rather than restyle it, [face blur](https://easyfaceai.com/photo-privacy) runs entirely in your browser and uploads nothing.
- The image models behind each look are listed openly on the [models page](https://easyfaceai.com/models).
